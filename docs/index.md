# Taking Book

Taking Book is a free, local-first PDF reading app for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. Your library stays on your device, and you can optionally keep your devices in sync through your own Google Drive.

## What it does

- Read PDF books as pages or as continuous, re-wrapped text, and pick up where you left off.
- Highlight passages and write notes, and find them again later across your whole library.
- Organise your Books with status, tags and favorites.
- Translate a selected word or phrase.
- Optionally sync your library, reading position, highlights and notes between your devices through a "Taking Book" folder in your own Google Drive. The app only accesses files it creates there.
